Looks up DNS and the registration record from your browser. It cannot load the site itself or prove why a domain stopped working.

What the states mean

Evidence, not attribution. Timeouts, HTTP errors, missing DNS and missing RDAP records do not prove a takedown or identify who acted. A registry hold identifies a registry/registrar role, but not its cause. Suspension pages and CDN warnings are indicators. Times are first observations at six-hour checks, measured approximately from a date-only report field.
What leaves your browser. The table is a static file published with this page. “Check a domain now” sends the domain you type to Cloudflare DNS (cloudflare-dns.com) and to rdap.org, which forwards it to the domain's registry. Nothing is stored.
